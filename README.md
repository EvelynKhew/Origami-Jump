## Project Overview
Molecular programming is a wide field of study that presents the world of computing with new materials in the form of DNA molecules to produce efficient computers. However, due to the complicated nature of this field, it can prove difficult to introduce and educate the concepts to beginners. DNA origami, a subfield of molecular programming, is the study of creating nanoscale structures using one long strand and multiple short strands of DNA molecules. Unlike other more established fields like linguistics or mathematics, DNA origami has yet to have a serious game developed for its education. This thesis intends to develop a game, Origami Jump, to teach the fundamentals of DNA Origami to nurture future molecular programmers, and determine whether Origami Jump can educate students more effectively when compared to a traditional lecture format. A between-subjects study design was adopted to compare Origami Jump with a traditional lecture format and tested for its efficacy. While Origami Jump did not prove to be more effective than a traditional lecture for direct learning, it was significantly more successful at student engagement. 

Origami Jump was developed using Unity and C#.
